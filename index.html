About Me

I am a trainer working across digital marketing, human resources, finance and soft skills. I plan the curriculum, deliver the sessions and assess the learners myself, so the training stays connected to what people need at work.

My sessions run on demonstrations, exercises and projects. I adjust the content for mixed-ability groups, whether they are college students or people already in jobs. I hold an MBA and a B.Com, and I have trained learners at institutes, colleges and through freelance assignments.
